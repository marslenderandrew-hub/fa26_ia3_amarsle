## Question 2: About My Process
When I first started the project, I had to decide how I wanted to begin. I chose to begin by making a giant prompt that gave it all the information needed to create the first iteration of the website. I chose to do it this way because I needed the simple fundamentals of the site to function. for example, I needed the multiple tabs and function of the site to work properly. Once I did that, I then just started to tune and add my information into it. This involved much smaller prompts but more of them to change the information and how it looks. This is where I spent most of the time on the project. My prompting strategy stayed the same as I was working because I was able to use smaller prompts to edit the small things. This let me focus on one issue at a time and fix that. I looked at the code and made sure it made sense. Although I don’t understand some of it because I am not very proficient in JavaScript. What I did was reiterate multiple times that the code and site must look professional. This way the AI made sure the code looked good and functioned correctly. Judging it was more difficult, but I did my best to look at it and try to understand what it was doing. Next project, I want to try to have the AI teach me how to do the commits and new branch parts of the project. This way I can learn that and won’t have to rely on AI when it comes to that. Other than that, I like my approach to the project.
